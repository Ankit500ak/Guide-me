## Project Overview

The Graduate Guidance and Rewards System is an innovative platform designed to facilitate the exchange of knowledge between graduates and newcomers. The system tracks mentorship activities, rewards contributions with star points, and allows graduates to redeem these points for various educational resources.

## Features

- **Mentoring System:**
  - Graduates can offer one-on-one mentoring sessions.
  - Juniors and newcomers can request guidance and book sessions.

- **Star Points:**
  - Accumulate star points based on contributions and mentoring activities.
  - Points can be tracked and managed through individual profiles.

- **Course Redemption:**
  - Redeem star points for online courses, books, and other educational materials.
  - Access a curated list of redeemable items and courses.

- **Profile Management:**
  - Create and update your profile with expertise areas and availability.
  - View your mentoring history, accumulated star points, and redeemed rewards.

- **Community Engagement:**
  - Participate in workshops, webinars, and events.
  - Engage with the community to earn additional rewards and recognition.

- **Feedback System:**
  - Provide and receive feedback on mentoring sessions.
  - Improve the quality of guidance through constructive feedback.

## System Architecture

- **Frontend:** 
  - Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  - Includes user interfaces for mentoring requests, profile management, and course redemption.

- **Backend:** 
  - Developed using Python (Flask/Django).
  - Handles authentication, mentoring session management, and star point calculations.

- **Database:** 
  - Uses a relational database (e.g., PostgreSQL, MySQL).
  - Stores user profiles, mentoring sessions, star points, and redemption records.

- **APIs:**
  - RESTful APIs for interaction between frontend and backend.
  - Endpoints for user management, mentoring requests, and rewards.

## How It Works

1. **Registration:**
   - Graduates and juniors/newcomers create accounts and set up profiles.
   - Graduates specify their areas of expertise and availability.

2. **Request Guidance:**
   - Juniors and newcomers browse available mentors and request guidance.
   - Mentoring requests are managed and scheduled through the platform.

3. **Conduct Mentoring Sessions:**
   - Graduates provide advice and support during scheduled sessions.
   - Sessions are logged and star points are awarded based on the level of engagement.

4. **Track and Redeem Rewards:**
   - Graduates track their accumulated star points and view available rewards.
   - Points can be redeemed for courses, books, and other resources.

## Contribution and Rewards

- **Mentoring Sessions:**
  - Earn 10 star points per session.
  - Additional points for extended sessions or exceptional guidance.

- **Providing Advice:**
  - Earn 5 star points per piece of advice or response.
  - Points awarded based on the quality and impact of advice.

- **Community Engagement:**
  - Earn 15 star points for organizing or participating in community events, workshops, or webinars.
  - Recognize and reward active contributors to the community.

- **Feedback and Improvement:**
  - Earn additional points for providing valuable feedback on sessions.
  - Contribute to the continuous improvement of the mentoring system.

## Guidelines for Graduates

- **Be Professional and Respectful:**
  - Provide guidance that is constructive and respectful.
  - Maintain professionalism in all interactions.

- **Keep Your Profile Updated:**
  - Regularly update your expertise areas and availability.
  - Ensure your profile reflects your current capabilities and interests.

- **Engage Actively:**
  - Participate in community events and contribute regularly.
  - Stay engaged to maximize your impact and rewards.

- **Provide Constructive Feedback:**
  - Offer helpful and actionable feedback to juniors and newcomers.
  - Use feedback to enhance the mentoring experience.
